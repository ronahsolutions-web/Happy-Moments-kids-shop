Our Story

Welcome to Happy Moments Kids Shopping Center! We are located in Lyantonde Mukanya and owned by Nimusiima Rebecca.

Our mission is to provide quality, affordable clothes, shoes and toys for children aged 0-10 years. We believe every child deserves to look good and feel happy.

Since opening, we have served hundreds of happy parents in Lyantonde and surrounding areas. We offer Cash on Delivery and friendly customer service.
